Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how quietthings ("we," "us," "our") handles information in connection with our website and our TikTok account (@quietthings6). We take privacy seriously and collect as little information as possible.

1. Information We Collect

From this website: We do not collect personal information, run analytics, set tracking cookies, or use third-party advertising trackers. The site is static HTML served by GitHub Pages, which may receive standard web server logs (IP addresses, timestamps) as part of normal operation.

From TikTok: Our automation tool authenticates to TikTok's official Developer API using OAuth 2.0 to post content to our own @quietthings6 account. The tool stores only the access and refresh tokens needed to publish to that account, locally on the operator's machine. We do not collect, store, or process data about TikTok viewers, followers, or commenters beyond what is publicly visible on the platform.

2. How We Use Information

OAuth tokens are used solely to:

  • Publish original videos and photos to the @quietthings6 TikTok account
  • Query basic profile information about our own account
  • Retrieve metadata about our own posts (e.g., view counts) for our own analytics

We do not sell, share, or transmit any data to third parties.

3. Data Retention

OAuth credentials are stored only as long as the project is active and are revoked or deleted when no longer needed. Tokens can be revoked at any time through TikTok's "Manage app permissions" page or by contacting us.

4. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Content published on TikTok follows TikTok's Community Guidelines.

5. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect OAuth credentials, including storing them only on the operator's local machine and never transmitting them to any party other than TikTok's official API endpoints.

6. Third-Party Services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which is subject to GitHub's privacy practices. Our integration uses TikTok's official Developer API, subject to TikTok's Privacy Policy.

7. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Ask what (if any) information we hold related to you
  • Request deletion of any information related to you
  • Ask questions about this policy

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, email quietthings.tiktok@gmail.com.
